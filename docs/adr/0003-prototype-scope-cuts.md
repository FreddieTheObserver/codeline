# Prototype scope cuts against interview evidence

Three things the stakeholders mentioned in session 1 are left out of the prototype on purpose, and the report lists them as future work.
The head-of-department overview (S1-HOD-11) is replaced by a Teaching assistant role with a read-only overview across all Courses and an export, because the team judged a TA preparing reports for the lecturer to be the more useful screen.
Onsite-only access by IP address (S1-LEC-04, S1-IT-02, "if possible") is deferred, because it is hard to demonstrate to a presentation audience, who may be on mobile data, and it isn't needed to show the exercise itself.
Roster import with one-time activation codes (S1-IT-05 asks students to verify who they are) is replaced by normal sign-up plus a Course Join code, which is simpler to build and lets the audience join the demo.

## Consequences

- Sign-up doesn't prove that a student ID belongs to the person typing it; the future roster import closes that gap.
- These are the first places to look if the lecturers ask why a stated need is missing from the prototype.
